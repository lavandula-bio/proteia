                      Your projects folder is synced to

                      Proteia keeps your projects in a folder that syncs, so uploads every project's files, the images you import included, to its cloud storage. Proteia itself sends nothing.

                      Unpublished data in your projects is then kept on that service's servers too, under its terms. If your lab or institution has rules on where unpublished research data may be kept, check that they allow this.

                      To keep projects off the cloud, change which folders syncs in its own settings; Proteia does not change them. That can move your projects, or leave them only in the cloud: afterwards, check that they are where the Projects dialog says they are saved. A setting to choose another projects folder is planned for a later version.
